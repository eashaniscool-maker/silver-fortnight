QUICK ANSWERS

Questions students ask about AI study tools

Are AI tools useful for studying?

Yes, when you use them to explain concepts, generate practice questions, or organize material and then verify the output. They are not a substitute for learning the material yourself.

How do I avoid inaccurate AI answers?

Check important claims against textbooks, lecture notes, or credible primary sources. Ask the tool to show uncertainty, and never treat a citation as real until you open and verify it.
